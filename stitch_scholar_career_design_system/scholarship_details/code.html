About the Scholarship

The Future Tech Innovators Scholarship is designed to support the next generation of leaders in computer science, software engineering, and artificial intelligence. We believe that financial barriers should never stand in the way of exceptional talent reaching its full potential.

This award not only provides significant financial backing but also includes a comprehensive mentorship program, pairing recipients with senior engineers at top-tier technology firms. Scholars will also receive invitations to our annual innovator summit in Silicon Valley.
